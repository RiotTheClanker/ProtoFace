## ProtoFace V6.2

| Board | File |
|---|---|
| **Mk2** (layout 11: 6 + 5 panels, 704 LEDs) | `ProtoFace-Mk2-V6.2.uf2` |
| **Mk3** (layout 14: 7 + 7 panels, 896 LEDs) | `ProtoFace-Mk3-V6.2.uf2` |

### Fixes
- **Mk2 face on the wrong panels.** The left chain was written reversed and the right
  chain's eye and mouth panels were swapped. The Mk2 now defaults to the panel order checked
  on the helmet: left `0 1 2 3 4 5`, right `3 4 0 1 2`. Mk3 is unchanged from V6.1.

### New
- `ident` shows each panel's position on its LED chain, so you can see how a helmet is wired.
- `map` shows the panel map; `map left …` / `map right …` change the panel order (and flip
  single panels) without reflashing. The map is saved in flash; `map reset` restores the default.
  See "Fixing the panel order without reflashing" in the README.

### Changes
- **New built-in fallback face** for both models: "NO SD" on the mouth and warning triangles
  on the eyes and nose, so a missing SD card or `start.anim` is obvious at a glance. The
  right side's text is mirrored to read correctly on the mirrored panels.

### Notes
- The `.anim` file format is unchanged.
